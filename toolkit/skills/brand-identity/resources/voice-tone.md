# Voice & Tone

Derived from copy actually shipped in `EW_Studio_Collections_V3.0`, not a generic
brand-voice template — every example below is real page copy verified in this
session, not invented.

## Headline register

Short, declarative, confident. No hedging, no exclamation points.

- `"Every Story Has a Sound."` — the site's primary hero headline.
- `"Three Collections. One Creative Ecosystem."` — section header, two short
  sentences rather than one long one.
- `"Choose Your Collection"` — plain imperative kickers above section headers.

Per this project's own `CHANGELOG.md` (a real, recorded design decision, not a
guideline invented after the fact): a heavy text-shadow glow was deliberately removed
from headings with the reasoning *"they're already pretty bold"* — the register favors
restraint over decoration once the typographic weight is already doing the work.
Tracking and weight were tightened at the same time (headline weight `800→700`),
another signal that this voice prefers confident-but-not-shouting over maximal.

## Tier/product copy

Each collection gets a one-line identity plus a two-line pitch, consistently:

- `ESSENTIAL — "Start here — and never outgrow it."`
- `CINEMATIC — "For the screen composer. Make every frame feel."`
- `EVERYTHING — "For the artist who won't be boxed in. Go all in."`

Pattern: a one-word or two-word badge naming who/what the tier is, then a short
sentence addressing the reader directly ("you"), then a second, punchier sentence.
Follow this shape for new tier or product copy rather than writing paragraph-length
descriptions.

## Where this voice does NOT apply

The project's own `README.md` is explicit that this is *"a design reference for the
web developer — not a production page and not wired to commerce."* Don't let
mockup-only affordances (cart, login, "Play" buttons) drift into being described or
built as real commerce copy/functionality unless a task explicitly asks for that.
